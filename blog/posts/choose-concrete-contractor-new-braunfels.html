Buying Guide

How to Choose a Concrete Contractor in New Braunfels

A concrete project is only as good as the planning and installation behind it. When comparing contractors in New Braunfels, focus on process and communication—not just the lowest number.

What to Look For

  • Site evaluation — Someone who reviews grade, drainage, access, and existing conditions before quoting.
  • Clear scope — Written understanding of what is included: removal, base, thickness, finish, and cleanup.
  • Realistic timelines — Weather and curing affect schedules; honest expectations are better than overpromising.
  • Attention to preparation — Base work and forming matter as much as the pour itself.
  • Local familiarity — Experience with Texas heat, local soils, and typical residential or commercial needs.
