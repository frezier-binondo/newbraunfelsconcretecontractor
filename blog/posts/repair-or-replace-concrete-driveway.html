When Should You Repair or Replace a Concrete Driveway?

Not every crack means the whole driveway must go. Not every damaged surface can be patched into long-term reliability. The right choice depends on the type and extent of damage and the condition of the base underneath.

When Repair Can Make Sense

  • Limited, stable cracks with a sound base
  • Minor surface wear or isolated edge damage
  • No significant settlement or heaving

When Replacement Is Often Better

  • Widespread cracking or map cracking
  • Noticeable settlement, low spots, or heaving
  • Base failure or chronic drainage problems under the slab
  • Surface that has scaled or deteriorated across large areas
