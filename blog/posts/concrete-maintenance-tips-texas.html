Concrete Maintenance Tips for Texas Homeowners

Concrete is durable, but it is not maintenance-free. A few habits help surfaces last longer under Texas sun, heat, and occasional freezes.

Practical Tips

  • Keep joints clear — Debris and vegetation in control joints can hold moisture and stress edges.
  • Address minor cracks early — Small issues are easier to manage than widespread damage.
  • Sealing — When appropriate, sealers can reduce staining and surface wear, especially on decorative finishes. Follow product and professional guidance.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals — Some cleaners and deicers can damage the surface.
  • Manage water — Fix drainage problems that leave water standing on or against the concrete.

If damage has already progressed, see repair options or ask us about an evaluation.
