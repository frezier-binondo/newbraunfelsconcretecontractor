How Long Does a Concrete Driveway Last?

A well-installed residential concrete driveway can serve for decades. In New Braunfels, heat, sun, occasional freezes, and local soils all influence how long the surface stays serviceable. Quality of the original installation remains the most important factor.

What Affects Lifespan

  • Base and thickness — Adequate compacted base and thickness for vehicle loads reduce settlement and cracking.
  • Reinforcement and joints — Proper reinforcement and control joints help manage natural shrinkage and movement.
  • Drainage — Water that ponds on or under the slab accelerates damage. Positive slope away from the house matters.
  • Traffic and loads — Heavy vehicles or repeated point loads stress the surface more than normal passenger cars.
  • Maintenance — Keeping joints clear, sealing when appropriate, and addressing minor issues early can extend service life.

Texas Climate Considerations

Summer heat and UV exposure affect curing and long-term surface condition. Occasional freeze-thaw cycles can stress concrete that has absorbed moisture. Installation practices that account for local weather—especially during placement and early curing—support better long-term performance.
