What Should You Know Before Installing a Concrete Slab?

A concrete slab is only as good as what is under it and how it is designed for use. Whether you need a garage floor, shop pad, or outdoor base, a few decisions up front affect long-term performance.

Key Considerations

  • Intended use — Cars, tools, storage, or outdoor living each place different demands on thickness and reinforcement.
  • Base — Stable, compacted support reduces settlement and cracking.
  • Drainage — Water should not pond on or under the slab.
  • Access — Truck and equipment access affect how the pour is planned.
  • Finish — Traction and cleanliness needs differ for garages versus outdoor slabs.

We do not provide structural engineering. Designs that require engineering should follow applicable codes and qualified professionals. Learn more on our concrete slabs page or request an estimate.
