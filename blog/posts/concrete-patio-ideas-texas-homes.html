Concrete Patio Ideas for Texas Homes

Outdoor living is a natural fit for New Braunfels and the Hill Country. A concrete patio can become the main gathering space for seating, dining, and grilling. Good planning makes the difference between a surface that feels unfinished and one that works year-round.

Layout and Use

Think about how you will use the space: dining table, lounge seating, outdoor kitchen, or a mix. Size the patio so furniture and walkways have room without feeling cramped. Connection to the house—doors, steps, and grade—should feel continuous and drain correctly.

Shade and Heat

Texas summers are intense. Plan for shade with a pergola, cover, or tree placement when possible. Lighter finishes can feel cooler underfoot than dark surfaces. Orientation relative to afternoon sun affects comfort in the hottest months.

Finish Options

  • Standard broom finish — practical traction and easy maintenance
  • Stamped concrete — patterns and color for a more decorative look
  • Borders and accents — define zones without full decorative treatment
