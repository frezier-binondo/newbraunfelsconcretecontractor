Helpful answers about concrete projects in New Braunfels and the surrounding area.

Project costs vary based on size, site conditions, preparation needs, materials, reinforcement, finish type, accessibility, and overall complexity. Fixed online prices rarely reflect real site conditions. We review the specific project and provide an estimate so you understand the scope and investment involved. Factors such as removal of existing concrete, steep grades, or decorative finishes can influence the total.
With proper installation—including adequate thickness, base preparation, reinforcement, joint placement, and drainage—a residential concrete driveway can perform well for decades. Local climate (heat, sun, occasional freezes), traffic load, and maintenance practices all influence longevity. Sealing and prompt attention to minor issues can help extend service life.
Concrete begins setting within hours and gains significant strength in the first days. Light foot traffic is often possible within 24–48 hours. Vehicle traffic on driveways is typically delayed longer—often several days to a week or more depending on mix, weather, and thickness. Full design strength continues to develop over weeks. We provide project-specific guidance on when the surface can be used.
