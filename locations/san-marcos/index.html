San Marcos sits between Austin and San Antonio along I-35, anchored by Texas State University and a growing residential base. Homeowners and property managers need concrete that handles daily use, Texas heat, and the drainage patterns common to the area. We provide concrete services for San Marcos properties with the same preparation standards we use in New Braunfels.

Concrete Needs in San Marcos

University-area rentals, family neighborhoods, and new development all create demand for durable driveways, outdoor living patios, garage slabs, and walkways. Outdoor entertaining is popular; many homeowners invest in concrete patios and stamped concrete finishes. Commercial and multi-unit properties need reliable flatwork and sidewalks that stand up to higher traffic.

Residential Applications

  • Concrete driveways — new, replacement, or extended
  • Patios and outdoor living spaces
  • Garage and utility slabs
  • Walkways and property access
  • Decorative stamped surfaces

Commercial and Multi-Unit

San Marcos commercial growth and student housing support ongoing need for slabs, sidewalks, and related flatwork. We coordinate with property managers and contractors when schedules and access require it. See our commercial concrete page for more detail.

Local Considerations

Summer heat affects placement and curing. Drainage toward local creeks and low areas should be planned into the grade. Soils can vary; base preparation is adjusted to site conditions rather than assumed uniform. These factors are reviewed on every San Marcos project.
