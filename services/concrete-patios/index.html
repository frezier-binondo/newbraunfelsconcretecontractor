Concrete Patio Installation in New Braunfels, TX

Outdoor living spaces, patio replacements, and extensions designed for Texas heat and everyday use.

Concrete patios expand usable outdoor space for seating, dining, grilling, and gathering. In New Braunfels, where outdoor living is a natural extension of the home, a well-planned patio becomes one of the most used areas of the property. We install new patios, replace deteriorated surfaces, and extend existing outdoor areas with attention to drainage, elevation, and how the space will be used.

New Patio Installation

A new concrete patio starts with the intended use: casual seating, outdoor dining, an outdoor kitchen base, or a multi-zone living area. We evaluate the yard grade, proximity to the house, existing drainage, and access for materials and equipment. Forms, base preparation, and reinforcement are set to create a level, durable surface that sheds water and connects cleanly to doors, steps, or other hardscape.

Finish options range from a standard broom texture for good traction to smoother or decorative treatments. Joint placement is planned to control cracking while keeping the overall appearance clean. Proper slope prevents water from pooling against the foundation or in seating areas.

Patio Replacement and Extensions

Older patios often show cracking, uneven settlement, or surface deterioration from years of sun and temperature changes. Replacement removes the failed concrete, corrects base issues where needed, and installs a new slab suited to current use. Extensions add square footage for larger gatherings or to connect separate outdoor zones. Matching elevations and finishes helps the new section integrate with what already exists.

Outdoor Living and Design Considerations

Many New Braunfels homeowners use concrete patios as the foundation for outdoor kitchens, seating walls, pergola posts, or pool surrounds. We coordinate thickness and reinforcement with those future elements when they are known at the planning stage. Decorative options such as stamped concrete can add pattern and color while retaining the strength of a solid concrete surface.

Texas weather places real demands on outdoor concrete. Intense summer sun, occasional heavy rain, and freeze-thaw cycles reward careful base work, adequate thickness, and good drainage. Shade, furniture placement, and access paths are also considered so the finished patio functions well day to day.

Key Elements of a Quality Patio

  • Site grading and drainage — Water directed away from the house and seating areas.
  • Stable base — Compacted material that supports the slab under expected loads.
  • Reinforcement — Appropriate for the span and any point loads from outdoor kitchens or heavy furniture.
  • Control joints — Planned locations to manage natural shrinkage and movement.
  • Finish texture — Traction underfoot while remaining easy to clean and maintain.
  • Curing protection — Especially important in hot New Braunfels summers.

Related Concrete Work

Patio projects often connect to other surfaces. Driveways, walkways, and repair of adjacent concrete can be coordinated. Decorative stamped finishes are a popular choice for patios and pool decks. We also serve homeowners in Schertz and Cibolo with the same focus on outdoor living performance.
