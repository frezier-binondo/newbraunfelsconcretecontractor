Concrete Sidewalk Installation in New Braunfels, TX

Residential walkways and commercial sidewalks with proper grading, drainage, and durable finishing.

Concrete sidewalks and walkways provide safe, defined access across a property. In New Braunfels they connect homes to the street, link outdoor living areas, and serve commercial properties and public-facing spaces. Proper grade, consistent width, and a durable finish make the difference between a surface that remains comfortable and one that becomes a maintenance problem.

Residential Sidewalks and Walkways

Homeowners typically need sidewalks for front-yard access, side-yard paths, or connections between the house, driveway, patio, and other outdoor features. We install new walkways and replace uneven, cracked, or settled sections. Elevation is coordinated with door thresholds, steps, and existing hardscape so the path feels continuous and drains correctly.

Width, alignment, and texture are chosen for everyday use. A broom finish provides reliable traction under wet conditions common after Texas rain. Control joints manage shrinkage so cracks occur in planned locations rather than randomly across the walking surface.

Commercial Sidewalks

Commercial properties require sidewalks that handle higher foot traffic, meet accessibility expectations, and integrate with building entries, parking, and landscape. We work on sidewalks for business properties, multi-unit sites, and construction projects. Coordination with other trades and attention to schedule are part of commercial flatwork delivery. Related commercial needs are covered on our commercial concrete page.

Grading, Drainage, and Accessibility

Sidewalks must shed water and avoid creating trip hazards. Positive drainage away from buildings and low spots is planned into the grade. Where the path meets the street, driveway, or other surfaces, transitions are kept smooth. Accessibility considerations—consistent slope, width, and surface texture—are addressed when the project requires them. We do not provide formal ADA design services; structural or accessibility designs that require engineering or code review should follow applicable requirements and qualified professionals.

Installation Process

  • Layout and elevation planning relative to existing structures and drainage.
  • Excavation and placement of a stable base.
  • Forming to the correct width, slope, and edge profile.
  • Reinforcement as appropriate for the span and expected use.
  • Concrete placement, finishing, and jointing.
  • Curing guidance and site cleanup.

Repair Versus Replacement

Localized damage can sometimes be addressed with concrete repair. Extensive cracking, significant settlement, or sections that no longer drain properly often favor replacement of the affected run. We evaluate the condition and discuss practical options so you can decide based on expected performance.

Serving the Area

We install and replace sidewalks in New Braunfels and nearby communities including Seguin and Kyle. Projects are planned around the specific site conditions and how the walkway will be used day to day.
